A little about us

A strong beginning for a bright future.

Early childhood is a special time to build curiosity, confidence, friendships, and a love of learning. Read Nursery School aims to provide a supportive environment where children can learn at their own pace and feel proud of who they are.

Through guided play, stories, creative activities, and caring relationships, we help children develop the skills they need for the next stage of their learning journey.
