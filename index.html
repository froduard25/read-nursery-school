ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A bright beginning for every child.

At Read Nursery School, we believe early childhood is a special time for curiosity, confidence and discovery. We aim to provide a welcoming environment where children can learn new skills, express themselves and grow together.

Through age-appropriate activities, creative play and caring guidance, we encourage children to explore the world around them and develop a love of learning.
