Our little world

A gentle start to a big, bright future.

Read Nursery School is a small, family-run nursery in the heart of Musanze, Rwanda. We believe the early years are for wondering, wandering, making a mess, and feeling truly at home.

Our play-led approach follows each child's natural curiosity. With gentle guidance, familiar faces and a whole lot of time outdoors, we help little people build confidence, kindness and a lifelong love of learning.

  • Play is the plan. Every activity is a tiny invitation to explore, question and discover.
  • Outside, every day. Fresh air, muddy boots and the changing seasons are part of our everyday rhythm.
  • Known and loved. Consistent key people mean every child is really seen, heard and celebrated.
